title: "Patna to Rameshwaram: To the Southern Tip"
slug: "patna-to-rameshwaram"

A solo highway ride cutting through the heart of the subcontinent: from the Gangetic plains of Bihar, across the Deccan plateau, through Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, down to the sea-swept Pamban Bridge and the edge of the Indian ocean in Rameshwaram.

Changing landscapes, languages, and weather within hours. Highway runs in 40-degree heat followed by monsoon squalls in the coastal south.
