title: "Patna to Ladakh: Solo Across the Passes"

Riding solo from Patna across the northern plains into the high mountain deserts of Ladakh.

Crossing high-altitude passes like Zoji La, Khardung La, and Chang La tests both the motorcycle and the mind. Cold starts at 4:00 AM, river crossings formed by melting glaciers, and rocky gravel tracks where every twist of the throttle requires full concentration.

The silence of Pangong Tso at sunrise and the vast expanse of More Plains make every kilometer worth the struggle.
